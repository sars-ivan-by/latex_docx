Roadmap

What 0.2.0 already does, and what is worth building next. This is not a release date. The same text lives in the repository as ROADMAP.md.

Shipped

  • A typical LaTeX article becomes a TD-J Word file. Formulas are Word math when the omml extra is installed.
  • Light Markdown uses the same DOCX builder.
  • Markdown becomes PowerPoint. An organization .pptx supplies size, theme, and layouts.
  • PDF is that DOCX saved by Microsoft Word.
  • A paragraph diff of two DOCX files.
  • Documentation in English, Russian, and Chinese.
  • lang="ru" or lang="en" on the library and the CLI. The default is Russian.
  • --docx-template keeps an organization's .docx or .dotx styles and page setup.
  • Section titles are Word heading styles. Markdown lists can nest, and [^id] becomes a Word footnote.
  • --template article is the one-block layout for a paper that is not a TD-J submission.

Next

Nothing further is queued. A full DOCX→TeX import stays out of scope. Word edits come back through the diff report.

Out of scope

  • Replacing pandoc.
  • Reconstructing the original LaTeX from an arbitrary DOCX.
  • A PDF renderer that does not go through Word. The PDF is supposed to match the DOCX.
